Shiplet is where teams share what their agents made, and how they made it.

Agents do much of the building now. The work itself turns into a link, a build,
or a file. The session that produced it, with the prompts, the agent's
reasoning, and every command and check, stays in one person's terminal and is
gone when they close it. Feedback lands in scattered threads, on a version of
the work that has changed six times since.

Shiplet keeps both together and makes both shareable:

- The **artifact** is the work: a build, prototype, document, file, or URL. It
  gets one review link where people comment on the work itself, and every
  comment keeps the version it was left on.
- The **trace** is the full agent session: prompts, replies, every tool call
  and result, screenshots, and videos. Secrets are masked on your computer
  before it leaves, and it stays private until you share it.

[Open the Shiplet app](https://shiplet.cc/), or ask your agent to share what it
just built.

## Decide exactly who sees what

Access is set per Shiplet and per trace, and the two are separate. Someone can
review the work without reading the session, or read a summary of the session
without seeing its tool output.

| You share | With | At |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An artifact | Yourself, your organization, anyone with the link, or the public; or one person, email address, or team | View or edit, granted separately |
| A trace | A person, an email address, a team, your organization, anyone with the link, or an API key | Full, Conversation, or Summary, with optional rules to mask terms, hide paths, hide reasoning, or hide videos |

The most specific audience wins, and **Preview as this audience** shows a trace
exactly as someone else will see it. Read [Access control](/docs/access-control)
and [Traces](/docs/traces).

## Works with every agent

Claude Code and Codex sessions can be shared from inside the agent: ask it to
"share this session to Shiplet". Any other agent can share a session by writing
the [open trace format](/docs/trace-format). Any agent that speaks MCP or can
run the CLI can load a shared trace and pick the work up where the last one
left off, whichever agent wrote it.

## Keep the context

The session sits next to the work it produced, so a reviewer can see how a
change was made and a teammate can continue it days later. Comments stay
attached to the revision they were left on, so "which version was that about?"
has an answer. Everything stays for as long as you keep the Shiplet;
[Traces](/docs/traces) lists the retention rules.

## Choose your task

- **[Share your first artifact and session](/docs/quickstart):** publish a
  build and attach the agent session that made it.
- **[Share an agent session](/docs/traces):** share the session you are in,
  then choose who can read it and at what level.
- **[Collect feedback](/docs/review-feedback):** leave comments on the work and
  work through review tickets.
- **[Manage access](/docs/access-control):** choose who can view or edit each
  Shiplet.
- **[Connect your agent](/docs/authentication):** sign in the CLI or MCP with
  OAuth, including from cloud agents with no browser.
- **[Connect a website](/docs/embed):** collect feedback on a live site.

Read [Why Shiplet](/docs/why-shiplet) for the idea behind the product.

## How it fits together

- A **Shiplet** owns the review link, its access, its revisions, its comments,
  and its traces.
- The **review layer** is the toolbar and trusted controls around the artifact.
  A team can replace it with a [custom widget](/docs/extensions) without
  changing the work under review.
- A **trace-only Shiplet** holds a session with no artifact, for work that has
  nothing to click on.

Artifact and widget code never receive reviewer credentials or direct access to
Shiplet's APIs. The trusted host keeps identity and every review action outside
the artifact frame.

## Automation

- **[CLI](/docs/cli):** `npx shiplet-cli` publishes artifacts, manages
  revisions, and shares and pulls traces from your terminal or agent.
- **[Code Mode MCP](/docs/code-mode-mcp):** connect any MCP client to publish,
  work with feedback, and load shared traces. Sign in with browser OAuth.
- **[REST API](/docs/api-keys):** use a narrowly scoped organization API key
  for CI and other unattended jobs.

The [OpenAPI document](https://shiplet.cc/openapi.json) is the machine-readable
contract for documented public REST operations.
